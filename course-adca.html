ADCA (Advanced Diploma in Computer Applications) — What is it?

Course Details, Duration, Fees & Career Opportunities

ADCA Course Introduction

ADCA is a 1-year professional computer diploma designed to make you job-ready.

Duration: 12 Months
Class Timing: 1 Hour Daily
Mode: Offline & Online Available
Eligibility: 10th Pass or Above
Certificate: NEXA Advanced Diploma Certificate
